# src/features/rent-roll

The /rent-roll page, in two views: Month (the month's rows per lease and KPIs from the summary) and Year (`?view=year&year=`, the client's spreadsheet rent roll from GET /rent-roll/year: one row per unit, or per lease with "By tenant" `?rows=lease`). Rent is billed automatically by the backend (Settings → Billing); there is no generate button. A row opens the lease page (`/leases/:id?tab=ledger&from=rent-roll&month=`). Charges, payments and the ledger itself live in `src/features/ledger`.

## keys.ts
- `rentRollKeys`: `all` (the shared `["rent-roll"]` prefix, so lease and money changes refresh both views), `month(month, propertyId)` and `year(year, propertyId, rowsBy)`.

## hooks.ts
- `rentRollQuery(month, propertyId)`: the GET /rent-roll query options (key + fetch), shared with the dashboard's month history.
- `useRentRoll(month, propertyId, { enabled }?)`: GET /rent-roll with month and the scope only (status and search are filtered on the client so counts and KPIs match).
- `useRentRollYear(year, propertyId, rowsBy)`: GET /rent-roll/year with `rows=unit` (one row per unit, its leases merged month by month) or `rows=lease` (one row per lease); property and grand totals are the same in both. Search and chips are filtered on the client. `data` is null when the response fails `isYearResponse` (an older backend), so the page can say so instead of crashing.

## stats.ts
- `monthFromParam(param)`: the ?month= value, or the current UTC month when missing or invalid.
- `isPastMonth(month)` / `isFutureMonth(month)`: before / after the current UTC month.
- `STATUS_FILTERS`, `StatusFilter`, `statusCounts(rows, upcoming)`: the segments (plus UI-only `UPCOMING`) and their counts; Not billed counts only rows the automatic billing will bill.
- `BillingKind`, `billingKind(dueDate, lease)`: how the automatic billing treats a lease this month (billable, starts-late, ends-early, not-monthly), same order as the backend (no proration).
- `UpcomingRow`, `upcomingCaption(kind, lease, month)`, `upcomingRows(rows, leases, month)`: NOT_BILLED rows the automatic billing skips, with their caption ("Starts Oct 14 · first rent Nov 1", "Ended Oct 20 · no October rent due", "Pays weekly · add charges by hand").
- `rowFilterStatus(row, upcoming)`: the row's segment (UPCOMING or its status).
- `rowSearchText(row)`: lease number, unit, primary tenant (rows don't carry co-tenants).
- `owingCount(rows)`: rows with balance > 0. `rowTotals(rows)`: billed count and previous balance / rent due / paid / owed (positive balances, matches the Outstanding KPI unfiltered) / credits sums in cents.
- `paidCaption(row)`: "incl. {amount} toward earlier balance" or "Covered by earlier credit" (payments pay the oldest charges first).
- `notBilledCaption(dueDate, pastMonth, leadDays)`: when a NOT_BILLED row bills (bill date = due date − lead days): "Bills automatically on Nov 11", "Bills on the next daily run" once the date is reached, "Due Nov 25 · bills automatically before then" without lead days (managers), "Not billed · add rent by hand" in a past month.
- `statusDate(row, pastMonth, leadDays)`: last payment date, "{n} days overdue" (`daysSince`) ("Unpaid at month end" in a past month), "Due {date}" or `notBilledCaption`.
- `rowLabel(row)`, `paymentTargets(rows)`: Record Payment choices (label, plus the tenant name, "Unit 2, 746 SPW LLC" and lease number for the searchable picker).
- `collectionBreakdown(rows)`, `CollectionBreakdown`: cents of the month's payments that went toward earlier balances (min(paid, max(opening, 0))) and that were paid in advance (max(0, paid - max(opening, 0) - charged)), over all rows.
- `depositAppliedNote(depositApplied)`: "incl. $X from deposits" under Total collected when deposits were applied this month, else null. `depositsHeldNote(pet, count, isCurrent)`: "incl. $X pet · N leases" (", as of today" off the current month) under Deposits held.
- `collectionNote(breakdown)`: "Includes {x} toward earlier balances and {y} paid in advance" (non-zero parts only), or null.

## yearStats.ts
Pure helpers for the yearly view (GET /rent-roll/year, one row per unit or per lease).
- `MIN_YEAR`, `currentYearUTC()`, `yearFromParam(param)`: ?year= from 2000 to next UTC year, else the current UTC year.
- `RentRollView`, `viewSwitchParams(prev, to, period)`: the URL after Month ⇄ Year (keeps ?q=, drops the other view's filters and ?rows=; month → its year; year → the current month this year, else January).
- `rowsFromParam(param)`, `rowsSwitchParams(prev, rowsBy)`, `rowsByLabels`, `tenantColumnHeader(rowsBy)`: the By unit | By tenant switch (?rows=lease; unit is the default and isn't written), its labels, and the first column's header ("Unit · Tenants" / "Tenant · Unit").
- `isUnitRow(row)`: a unit row (has `leases`) vs a lease row. `isYearResponse(data, rowsBy)`: the response has the shape this page knows for that mode (rowsBy echoed, 12 months, totals, 12 cells per row; unit rows with `leases` and `leaseIds`). `OUTDATED_BACKEND_TITLE` / `OUTDATED_BACKEND_DESCRIPTION`: the message shown when it doesn't.
- `shortMonth(month)` / `shortMonthYear(month)`: "Oct" / "Oct 2026" (headers, tooltips), no Date math.
- `CELL_STATUSES`, `LEGEND_STATUSES`, `cellStatusLabels`, `cellTone`: every status; the legend's (Paid, Partial, Unpaid, Overdue, Vacant; not billed / future / outside lease are plain empty or "-" cells); friendly labels (rent statuses from StatusBadge) and theme classes per status (`cell` for the table cell and strip square, `swatch` for the legend). Problems stand out: OVERDUE strongest (red tint, red bold text, left marker), UNPAID red, PARTIAL amber; PAID calm (a light success tint, stronger in dark mode so it stays faintly visible); VACANT light stripes.
- `VACANT_STRIPES`, `RESERVED_STRIPES`, `reservedTone`, `toneOf(cell, reserved?)`: diagonal stripes from theme tokens (`repeating-linear-gradient`); a reserved unit's months use another neutral shade and direction.
- `monthCellClass(cell, reserved?)`: a cell's classes (SpreadsheetTable `cellClassName`). `shownCell(row, cell)`: by tenant, a lease row's VACANT month shows as outside that lease (a muted "-"); vacant / reserved units keep their stripes.
- `cellDescription(cell, opts)`: the cell's tooltip and screen-reader text: "Mar 2026 · John Smith · Charged $2,000.00 · Rent paid $2,000.00 · Cash received this month $0.00 · Paid" (Remaining and Balance only when not zero; the status is the month's own). `chargedLine(m)`: the "Charged · Rent paid · Cash received this month" part, also the monthly rows' tooltip.
- `cellDisplay(cell)`: what a cell shows: the paid amount in short money ("$2,000", "$2,150.50"), "" for vacant / reserved (stripes) and unpaid future months, or null for a Dash (outside a lease, not billed). `cellDescription(cell, { reserved, tenants })`: the tooltip / screen-reader text with exact amounts and the month's tenant ("Mar 2026 · John Smith · Paid $2,000.00 of $2,000.00 · Paid"; two leases "John Smith → Sarah Lee"; OVERDUE ends "Overdue · balance includes earlier months"; "Vacant" / "Reserved" / "No lease this month").
- `isCurrentMonth(month)`, `isQuarterEnd(index)`: the "Now" column (current UTC month) and the thin lines after Mar, Jun, Sep.
- `leaseTenantLabel(lease, withCount?)`: the primary tenant ("+n" co-tenants with `withCount`).
- `cellTenants(row, cell)`: who a month belongs to: a unit row from `leaseIds` via `leases` ("John Smith → Sarah Lee"); a lease row its own tenant in that lease's months.
- `monthRange(months)`: "Jan–Apr" / "Mar". `otherLeases(row)`: the unit's other leases split into `earlier` / `later` around the current one (empty `monthsCovered` left out: only owing from an earlier year). `tenantsWithMonths(leases)`: "John Smith (Jan–Apr), Sarah Lee (Apr–Jun)" (the CSV's Earlier tenants column; the UI doesn't show earlier tenants).
- `isLeaseEnded(row)`, `daysToEndText(row)`: "Ends in 52 days" / "Ends tomorrow" / "Ends today", or "Ended Nov 30, 2026" (the day it really ended: `endedOn`, else `endDate`), "Cancelled Nov 20, 2026" for a cancelled lease (never a negative number). The Lease end column and the CSV use `leaseEndDate` too.
- `groupCountText(rows)`: "4 units", or by tenant "4 units · 8 leases" (rows are leases; a renewal is a second lease of the same tenant), for the bands and the phone property cards.
- `shownRenewalAlert(row)`: the alert the table's first column shows ("Ended" only by unit; the CSV keeps every alert).
- `renewalAlertLabels`, `renewalAlertTone`, `renewalText(nextLease)`: "Ends soon" / "Ended" badges; "Renewed" / "Renewal drafted" / "Next tenant" (the backend's wording).
- `isReservedRow(row)`, `rowTitle(row)`, `shownVacantMonths(row)`: a VACANT_UNIT row whose unit is RESERVED (e.g. the owner's office) reads "Reserved" (grey badge), its cells say "Reserved", its vacant months are hidden and the Vacant chip skips it; other unit rows read "Vacant".
- `tenantNames(row)`, `yearRowKey(row)`, `yearSearchText(row)`: the current lease's tenants joined, the row key (the unit; a lease row its lease), the texts search looks in (a unit row: every tenant and lease number of the unit's year, earlier ones too; a lease row: its own lease; "Unit 4B"; property; "Vacant" / "Reserved").
- `YearChip`, `YEAR_CHIPS`, `yearChipLabels`, `chipsFromParam(param)`: the Ends soon / Unpaid months / Vacant chips kept in ?chips=.
- `hasUnpaidMonths(row)`, `hasVacancy(row)` (a VACANT_UNIT row or any VACANT month; never a reserved row), `matchesChips(row, chips)`, `filterYearRows(rows, search, chips)`: the filters (every active chip narrows).
- `PropertyGroup`, `groupByProperty(rows, propertyTotals)`: filtered rows per property in the API's order, with that property's API totals.
- `yearLeasePath(leaseId, year, tab?)`: `/leases/:id?[tab=ledger&]from=rent-roll&view=year&year=` (the lease page links back to this year).
- `collectedPercent(paid, charged)` (one decimal, integer cents; null when nothing charged), `collectedLabel(pct)` ("97%", rounded down below 100), `collectedTone(pct)` (100%+ success, 90%+ warning, else danger): the Collected column and KPI.
- `yearSummary(rows)`: vacant unit-months (not reserved), leases ending soon, units with unpaid months, over every row in scope; each unit counted once, so both modes give the same numbers.
- `YEAR_OPTIONAL_COLUMNS`, `YearOptionalColumn`, `YEAR_OPTIONAL_KEYS`: the Columns menu choices (Market rent, Lease start, Lease end, Deposit, Late fees, Vacancy months, Days to lease end, Renewal), all hidden by default.

## rentRollCsv.ts
Shared rules for both CSV exports.
- `csvMoney(value)`, `centsToCsv(cents)`, `csvText(value)`, `csvField(value)`: money as plain numbers ("3400.00", no float math), a leading `'` on text that would run as a formula, quoting.
- `csvLines(lines)`: CSV text with CRLF line ends. `propertySuffix(name?)`: " - {property}" without characters Windows forbids. `saveCsv(csv, filename)`: saves with a UTF-8 BOM (Excel).

## monthCsv.ts
- `MONTH_CSV_HEADER`, `buildMonthCsv(data)`: the whole month for the scope from the loaded GET /rent-roll response (search, status and sort ignored), one line per lease in the API's order: Property, Unit (`unitLabel`), Tenant (primary only: the monthly rows have no co-tenants), Lease number, Lease status, Rent, Due date, Previous balance, Charged, Paid, Balance, Status, Oldest unpaid due date, Last payment, Deposit held; then a "Total" line summing the four money columns in cents (`rowTotals`, like the table's Totals row). "Rent paid" and "Remaining" come after "Paid" (summed on the Total line in cents).
- `monthCsvFilename(month, propertyName?)`: "Rent roll November 2026.csv", plus " - {property}". `downloadMonthCsv(data, propertyName?)`: saves it (BOM).

## yearCsv.ts
- `yearCsvHeader(months, rowsBy)`, `buildYearCsv(data)`: the whole year (search and chips ignored), following the response's `rowsBy`: one line per unit in the API's order (Unit as `unitLabel`; with an "Earlier tenants" column: "John Smith (Jan–Apr)", and "Current rent"), or one line per lease (no Earlier tenants; "Rent"), a "Subtotal" line after each property (from `propertyTotals`) and a "Total" line (from `totals`); vacant / outside-lease months are blank. "Rent paid {year}" and "Remaining {year}" after the Late fees column: each row's 12 cells summed in cents; on subtotal and total lines summed over those rows (the API's totals don't have them).
- `yearCsvFilename(year, propertyName?, rowsBy?)`: "Rent roll 2026.csv", "Rent roll 2026 by tenant.csv", plus " - {property}" for one property.
- `downloadYearCsv(data, propertyName?)`: saves it with a UTF-8 BOM (`saveCsv`).
